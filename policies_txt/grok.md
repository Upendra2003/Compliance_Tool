## 13. Consumer FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions)

### Data Collection and Usage
**Q: What data does xAI collect from users?**
A: We collect various types of data as described in Section 2 of this Privacy Policy, including Account Data, User Content, Technical Data, and Feedback Data. This helps us provide, maintain, and improve our Service.

**Q: Is my conversation data used to train Grok?**
A: Yes, conversations may be used to train and improve our models, unless you have Private Chat enabled. With Private Chat turned on, your conversations are deleted from our systems within 30 days (unless required for legal, compliance, or safety purposes).

**Q: Can I opt out of having my data used for training?**
A: You can use Private Chat mode to prevent your conversations from being used for training purposes. However, other data may still be used as described in this Privacy Policy.

### Data Sources for Training
**Q: What sources does xAI use to train Grok?**
A: We use publicly available information from the internet, licensed datasets, and user interactions (with appropriate safeguards and anonymization). We do not actively seek sensitive personal information.

**Q: Does xAI use personal information from the web for training?**
A: While we use publicly available data that may incidentally contain personal information, we implement measures to minimize the inclusion of sensitive personal data and do not process data to infer sensitive characteristics about individuals.

### Data Control and Management
**Q: How can I delete my conversations?**
A: You can delete individual conversations through the Service interface, or delete your entire account. Deleted data is removed from our systems within 30 days, unless retention is necessary for legal, compliance, or safety purposes.

**Q: What happens when I delete my account?**
A: Account deletion removes your personal information from our active systems within 30 days. Some data may be retained for legal, compliance, or safety purposes as described in Section 5.

### Model Behavior and Output
**Q: Can Grok generate inaccurate information about me?**
A: Yes, like all AI models, Grok may generate outputs that contain factual inaccuracies. If output contains factually inaccurate personal information about you, you can submit a correction request, though correction may not always be technically feasible.

**Q: How does xAI handle biased or harmful outputs?**
A: We implement safety measures and content filtering to reduce harmful outputs, but no system is perfect. Users can provide feedback through the rating system to help us improve.
